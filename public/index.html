Questions

The things people ask first.

Does my customer need an app?
No. Tapping a tag is built into the phone itself. On an iPhone the review page appears as a banner to tap; on Android it usually opens straight away.
Which phones work?
Every iPhone from the XR onwards reads tags without unlocking anything, and effectively all Android phones sold in the last several years do too. If a phone is very old, the tag simply does nothing — there is no error and no confusion.
Can I choose which reviews I get?
No, and neither can we. The tag opens your public Google review page, and whatever the customer writes is theirs. Filtering or gating reviews is against Google's policies and risks your listing. The tag makes it easier to ask everyone — it does not choose who answers.
What if I move premises or rebrand?
Get in touch and we will re-program your tags to the new listing free of charge. The tag is not printed with the link, so it can be pointed somewhere new whenever you need.
Does it need power, wifi or a subscription?
None of the three. The tag has no battery and no connection — the phone powers it for the instant it is held nearby. You buy it once.
How long does setup take?
The tags arrive ready to use. Putting one on your counter is the whole installation, and we will do it with you if we are dropping them off in person.
