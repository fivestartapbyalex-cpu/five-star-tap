Why it matters

The moment passes in about four seconds.

A customer is happiest right after you have served them, standing at your counter with their phone already in their hand. That is the only moment they will ever leave you a review, look at tonight's specials, or follow you. Then they walk out.

Asking out loud is the hard part.

Staff forget. Customers mean to and never do. A card with a web address gets left on the table, and a QR code means opening a camera, lining up a square and hoping it focuses.

A tag removes every one of those steps. Hold a phone near it and the page is already open.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Does my customer need an app?
No. Tapping a tag is built into the phone itself. On an iPhone the page appears as a banner to tap; on Android it usually opens straight away.
Which phones work?
Every iPhone from the XR onwards reads tags without unlocking anything, and effectively all Android phones sold in the last several years do too. If a phone is very old the tag simply does nothing — there is no error and no confusion.
Which platforms can a tag point at?
Google, Yelp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp and Snapchat, plus your own menu or website. A tag holds one link at a time, so one tag does one job — that is why most people start with two.
Do you print my business name on it?
Not as standard. The tags come pre-printed with clean artwork, and what makes one yours is the link inside it. That is what keeps them $40 and shipping in two days rather than two weeks. If you want custom printing for a larger order, ask and we will quote it.
How long until it arrives?
Programmed and shipped within two days of your order, then normal post from New York. It arrives ready to use — putting it on your counter is the whole installation.
What if I move premises or change my listing?
Get in touch and we will re-program it free. Nothing is printed on the tag, so it can be pointed somewhere new whenever you need — and we can walk you through doing it yourself over the phone.
Can I choose which reviews I get?
No, and neither can we. The tag opens your public review page, and whatever the customer writes is theirs. Filtering or gating reviews is against Google's policies and risks your listing. The tag makes it easier to ask everyone — it does not choose who answers.
Does it need power, wifi or a subscription?
None of the three. The tag has no battery and no connection — the phone powers it for the instant it is held nearby. You buy it once.
